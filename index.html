Librain Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

Scope and contact

This policy covers Librain for Android (com.greenkeycompany.librain), including older versions and the redesigned Librain Astral release. For privacy questions, contact the Librain developer at greenkeyuniverse@gmail.com. The sections below distinguish the redesigned app from older versions with advertising and online ranking.

Training data stays on your device

The redesigned app stores exercise settings, answers and results, accuracy, response times, training dates, streaks, personal records, adaptive difficulty profiles, expedition checkpoints, opened stars, earned stardust, appearance purchases made with stardust, onboarding state and your preferences locally. These records power the game and your statistics. The redesigned Librain app has no developer-operated backend, account system, cloud game sync or online leaderboard. We do not send training records to our servers.

Older versions: advertising and Google Play Games

Older Librain versions include Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), Google Play Games and Google Play purchases. These integrations are absent from the redesigned app except for Google Play purchases. Installing the redesigned version stops its use of AdMob and Google Play Games; it does not automatically delete information previously processed by Google.

Advertising. Older versions initialize Google's advertising SDK and request banner or interstitial ads. Advertising requests and the SDK can send Google technical data, including IP address (which may indicate approximate location), advertising/device identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information. Google uses such information to deliver and measure advertising, operate its services and detect fraud. The data and processing depend on the installed SDK version, device settings and Google's services. This does not mean Librain requests precise GPS location or receives the full advertising profile. The developer may receive advertising performance reports through AdMob. Google handles advertising data under its own policies. You can manage advertising privacy options or the advertising identifier in Android/Google settings where available; this does not guarantee deletion of earlier records.

Online ranking and Google account. Older versions connect to Google Play Games using your Google gaming profile when the service connection is available. At the end of a ranked run, the app submits the reached stage as a score to Google's leaderboard. Google can associate it with the gaming profile and process profile identifiers and service information. The leaderboard may show your gaming name, profile image and score to other players according to your Google Play Games privacy settings. The app does not send the full exercise answer history through this score submission. A Google gaming profile is separate from an account operated by the Librain developer. Manage profile visibility, game connections and deletion of gaming data in Google Play Games/Google account settings.

Local progress and purchases. Older versions also keep campaign progress, personal bests, preferences and paid-access status on the device, and use Google Play to process purchases. Clearing local app data does not remove Google-held leaderboard, advertising or purchase records. For questions about data controlled by the developer, email greenkeyuniverse@gmail.com; data held independently by Google is subject to Google's controls, retention rules and privacy policy.

Google Play and Astral access

Google Play handles store purchases and restoration. Librain receives purchase information such as the product identifier, purchase token, purchase state, acknowledgement state and signed receipt, which may contain an order identifier and transaction time. The app checks this information locally and stores an access status and any information needed to retry confirmation with Google Play. The original paid-access flag may be imported when you update. Google Play may process account, device and transaction information under its own policies. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. Uninstalling Librain does not delete your Google Play purchase history or cancel an entitlement. Purchase confirmation and restoration can require an internet connection.

Android backup and device transfer

Android may back up or transfer local game data according to your device and Google account settings. This is an operating-system feature, not Librain cloud synchronization. In the redesigned app, the dedicated Astral entitlement cache is excluded by app backup rules. Other preferences, including historical legacy preferences, may be governed by system backup. A system backup can restore data after reinstalling.

Optional reminders and permissions

In the redesigned app, training and streak reminders are scheduled on the device. Notification permission is requested when needed and is optional; you can disable reminders in Librain or notifications in Android settings. Librain does not use a developer push-notification service. Haptic feedback may use vibration. The app does not request contacts, location, microphone or camera access for its gameplay.

Sharing reports and external links

In the redesigned app, if you choose to export statistics, the app creates a PDF in its private cache and opens the Android share chooser. The report is shared only through the app you select; that app has its own data practices. Exported copies can remain with you or the recipient after deletion in Librain. Temporary report files are managed locally and can be removed through cache clearing. Links to astronomy information or this policy open external websites, whose operators may process technical information such as your IP address.

Advertising, analytics and support

The redesigned app includes no advertising SDK, behavioral analytics SDK or third-party crash-reporting SDK. Google Play and Android may independently process store, diagnostic and security information under their settings and policies. If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information or attachments you send, uses them to respond and resolve the request, and retains them only as needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. Please do not send passwords, signing keys, payment-card details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Retention, security and deletion

Local records remain while the app data is retained on your device. You can remove local records using Android Settings → Apps → Librain → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Clearing only the cache does not delete progress. Manage Android backups separately in your system/account settings if you do not want them restored. There is no developer-operated Librain account to delete. Clearing storage or uninstalling does not delete older Google Play Games or advertising records; use the relevant Google service controls or contact Google for those records. Local data is held in app-private storage protected by Android; this is not a promise of additional database encryption. Google Play controls its purchase records and communications. No method of storage or transmission guarantees absolute security.

Children and medical information

Librain is a memory and attention game. It does not diagnose or treat medical conditions, measure IQ or require health information. The developer does not knowingly solicit children’s personal information through gameplay. The older advertising and gaming SDKs can process technical and profile information as described above; this is not a claim that those versions collect no children’s data. A parent or guardian may contact the developer if a child has sent personal information to support, so the request can be reviewed and appropriate deletion arranged.

Changes and third-party policies

This policy will be updated when the app’s data practices change. The effective date identifies the version. Google’s privacy policy applies to relevant Google services; GitHub’s privacy statement applies when you visit this policy hosted on GitHub Pages. The site uses no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies set by Librain or remote web fonts. Hosting providers may retain technical request logs under their policies.
